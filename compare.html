    Each term is a whole-word search, in any order, case and accents aside. OR between alternatives counts headlines with any of them (congo OR drc). site:bbc.com after a term counts one source, and its share is then of that source's headlines.

    Try: ukraine, gaza, sudan; covid against flu, as a share; trump on bbc.com against nytimes.com; el niño, drought, floods, each at its peak.

      A story is a headline's first appearance anywhere in a week; an outlet, its first appearance on each site; an article, every page that carried it. A share is of the same measure over every headline GDELT collected that month, or over the source's. GDELT's set of sources is not constant (between 40 and 53 million articles a year since 2020, from a changing mix of sites), so a share across years is against a moving base; a term's share of one source's headlines is against that source's own output.
