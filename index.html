Headline Search

News headlines as recorded by the GDELT Global Knowledge Graph, which has logged the title of every English-language article it sees since October 2019, about half a billion so far. Working out what is loaded…

Word search matches whole words, in any order. Substring search matches the exact run of characters, so zelensk finds both spellings. Case does not matter. Oldest first is how you find when a phrase first turned up in the news.



Try: ceasefire, climate change, zelensk (substring), election on bbc.com, tariff, oldest first.

Data is GDELT's, ingested a few times a day, so the last day is always incomplete. Headlines are page titles as GDELT recorded them, site name suffix and all. Click a source to narrow to it, or a month in the count to narrow to that month. Source.
